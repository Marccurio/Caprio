About Caprio & Co

Caprio & Co is a metal exporter and supplier specializing in Ferro Titanium, ferro alloys, industrial scrap, and titanium alloy products. We work through strong manufacturer tie-ups to provide high-grade materials in multiple forms and specifications. Our focus is on consistent quality, customized supply solutions, and dependable service for domestic and international buyers.

  • Export-quality ferro alloys and titanium products
  • Wide product range available under one source
  • Reliable quality and material sourcing
  • Serving clients across India and overseas
