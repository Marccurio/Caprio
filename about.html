Company overview

Specialists in Ferro Titanium and specialty alloys

Caprio & Co founded in 2022 and headquartered in Mumbai, India, Caprio & Co is a professional supplier of Ferro Titanium and specialty titanium materials for the metallurgical and industrial sectors. We work through direct manufacturer tie-ups, enabling us to provide reliable supply, consistent quality, and customized material solutions tailored to customer requirements.

Our core strength lies in Ferro Titanium, offered in multiple forms and grades to meet diverse industrial applications. We are committed to delivering top-quality materials from India without compromise, supported by disciplined management, prompt communication, and strong follow-up throughout every stage of business.

While our primary market is India, we are actively engaged with customers from various countries and continue to expand our international business network. At Caprio & Co, we believe long-term partnerships are built on quality, transparency, responsiveness, and dependable service.
